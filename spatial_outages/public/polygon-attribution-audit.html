The proposed flow

Wide when proven. Local when it is not.

The parent outage remains the source record. Sub-outages add the missing local layer.

1 · Keep

Parent outage

Preserve the original outage ID, trigger time and member devices.

2 · Check

Is the CSP down widely?

Use CSP health, peer signals and affected share.

Yes → keep the CSP-wide verdict
3 · Cluster

If no, find local pockets

Group nearby failures using space, time and network context.

4 · Send

Attribute every device

Give each pocket a cause, confidence and message for CSP and customer.

Communication

Send the local verdict to the people who need it.

Each device inherits its sub-outage ID, verdict, confidence and next update.

For CSP operations
“Outage 3000002606-A: 5 nearby devices form one local pocket. Parent outage retained. Local cause, evidence and confidence attached.”
For affected customers
“We see a local service issue near your device. We do not currently see a provider-wide outage. We will update you as this local issue changes.”
