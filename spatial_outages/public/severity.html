02 · Verified source

Minimal input, derived evidence

One V3 table supplies the raw membership fact. Geography and eligible-device denominators come from historical inventory because affected counts alone cannot prove that a zone is down.

SELECT outage_id, device_id, csp_id, member_first_fail_at_ist
FROM PROD_DB.BUSINESS_EFFICIENCY_ROUTER_OUTAGE_DETECTION_PUBLIC.OUTAGE_MEMBER_V3
WHERE NOT COALESCE(_fivetran_deleted, FALSE);

03 · Meaning

Never mix these fields

A severe outage can still have an unknown cause. A likely cause can still have weak evidence. Keep all three dimensions explicit.

01 / IMPACT

Severity

How many customers are affected, what share they represent, and for how long.

02 / DIAGNOSIS

Cause

The most likely failure domain: backend, power, fibre, OLT, ISP, or unknown.

03 / EVIDENCE

Confidence

How reliable the detection and attribution are based on validation and corroboration.

07 · Build plan

Shortest path to bucketing

Build only the evidence required by the tree. The existing strict H3 label and the proposed 15/30-minute operational trigger remain separately named outputs.

Prepare evidence

  • Extract four-column member fact
  • Attach historical H3
  • Build CSP-H3 eligible denominators
  • Aggregate affected count and share

Classify and publish

  • Mark DOWN, UP, or UNKNOWN
  • Match temporally overlapping outages
  • Apply the first matching tree rule
  • Emit outage ID, bucket, confidence
Skipped for v1: TICK_LEDGER ingestion, recovery modelling, probability models, and unverified physical-cause claims.
≥80%

Validation constraint: tune only minimum affected devices, minimum affected share, temporal overlap, and the “almost all zones” fraction. Choose the simplest values that capture at least 80% of known outages while minimizing false promises.
