How we turn silent routers into outage decisions

A router going silent is a warning, not proof of a shared outage. We compare failures across time, location, and network operators before we publish an incident.

Goal: at least 80%of known outages found, with fewer false alarms.

Problem

A router may stop reporting because of power, connectivity, hardware, maintenance, or a data problem. We need enough supporting evidence to tell a real shared outage from an isolated failure.

Requirements

Watch earlyFlag a possible issue after 15 minutes.
Confirm carefullyTreat 30 minutes of silence as usable evidence.
Use the right placeUse the router's location when it failed.
Compare fairlyCompare failed routers with active routers in that area.
Keep the source IDDo not replace the existing outage ID.
Admit uncertaintyReturn Unknown when comparison data is missing.

Exact ordered rule contract

The engine is deterministic. Only these four values are tuned; the rule order and fallback are fixed.

MIN_AFFECTED_DEVICESMinimum failed routers in one CSP–H3.
MIN_AFFECTED_SHAREMinimum failed ÷ eligible routers in that CSP–H3.
OVERLAP_WINDOWMaximum time gap for grouping source outages into one comparison event.
ALMOST_ALL_H3_SHAREMinimum affected H3s ÷ eligible H3s for a CSP-wide event.

Stage A — assign a state to every CSP–H3 row

DOWNHistorical H3 and denominator exist, affected devices meet both minimums: count and share.
UPHistorical H3 and denominator exist, observations are usable, and the DOWN condition is false.
UNKNOWNHistorical H3 is missing or ambiguous, the eligible denominator is missing, or the observation cannot be compared safely.

Stage B — apply these outage rules from top to bottom

PriorityIFTHENEvidence used and retained
1 Valid evidence exists, but no CSP–H3 row passes the DOWN count-and-share gate. NOISE Affected devices, eligible devices, affected share, H3 mapping status, and the failed gate.
2 Within OVERLAP_WINDOW, two or more CSPs are DOWN in the same H3, and those CSPs are not DOWN in their other H3s. AREA-SHARED Compared CSP count, shared H3, overlap times, and each CSP's other-H3 states.
3 Within OVERLAP_WINDOW, two or more CSPs are DOWN in the same H3, and those CSPs are also DOWN in other H3s. REGIONAL Compared CSP count, affected H3s by CSP, eligible H3s by CSP, and overlap times.
4 The same-H3 multi-CSP test is false, and one CSP is DOWN in at least ALMOST_ALL_H3_SHARE of its eligible H3s. ISP / OLT Affected H3 count, eligible H3 count, affected-H3 share, and same-H3 CSP comparison.
5 The same-H3 multi-CSP test is false, the CSP-wide test is false, and at least one neighboring CSP in the same H3 is observed UP. LOCAL OPERATOR FAULT Affected CSP state, its other-H3 states, neighboring CSP states, and comparison freshness.
6 A required comparison is missing, or the observed pattern is conflicting or ambiguous. This includes a single-zone CSP or a single-CSP H3. UNKNOWN The missing comparison and all available evidence; never replace it with a guessed cause.

First matching rule wins. Preserve every source outage_id; attribution_event_id only groups overlapping outages for comparison. The bucket is a likely failure domain, not proof of a power, fibre, ISP, or OLT root cause.

Published cause buckets

Every outage receives one bucket. Confidence is reported separately.

NOISEThere is not enough evidence to call this a shared outage.
AREA-SHAREDSeveral operators are down in one zone, but not across their other zones.
REGIONALSeveral operators are down in the same zone and across other zones.
ISP / OLTOne operator is down across all or almost all of its zones.
LOCAL OPERATOR FAULTOne operator is down in a zone while neighboring operators remain up.
UNKNOWNThe required comparison is missing or the pattern is ambiguous.

How to read the bucket dry runs

  • CSP Alpha: provider being evaluated.
  • CSP Beta: comparison provider in the same H3.
  • Names: examples, not real companies.
  • Minimum evidence: 5 failed routers and 70% affected.
  • Time match: failures overlap within 30 minutes.
  • Almost all zones: at least 80%. Validate all values before production.
