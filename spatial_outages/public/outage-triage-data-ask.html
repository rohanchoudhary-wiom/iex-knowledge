1. Contract

Detection decides what is down and freezes the affected devices. Labs explains the likely cause and confidence. Communication decides what to tell each audience.

  • outage_id and its device list are source truth.
  • The one-outage-to-one-polygon mapping is a locked requirement.
  • Labs never changes membership, splits an outage, merges outage IDs, or creates another published ID.
  • Labs may compare overlapping or concurrent outages internally, but overlap is evidence only.
  • Overlap never creates another outage, polygon, output row, or attribution.
  • Attribution is always published against the original outage_id.
  • The result may be revised while open and finalised after restoration.

Detection v0 assumption: a device was silent for 15 minutes, had a SUCCESS in the previous 30 minutes, and joined devices that went silent inside the same 30-minute onset window. A changed rule requires a versioned interface update.

2. Triage output taxonomy

Each outage has exactly one current root cause.

CSP_SIDE

Upstream ISP or OLT-shaped failure, merged for v1.

ACCESS_FIBRE

Local access-fibre failure.

PREMISE_POWER

Power or shared electrical failure.

UNKNOWN

Evidence cannot support a reliable cause.

CSP_SIDE retains a nullable sub_cause for a future ISP/OLT split.

Separate dimensionValues
spatial_extentLOCAL, SUB_REGIONAL, REGIONAL
event_patternUNPLANNED, SCHEDULED, RECURRING, UNKNOWN

Regional and sub-regional describe scale, not root cause.

3. Labs flow

Load one outage and its frozen devices.
Build exactly one polygon and calculate area.
Enrich from portfolio-wide history.
Compare concurrent outages internally.
Calculate cause, confidence, and restoration class.
Upsert one versioned result per outage_id.

5. Polygon and device counts

  • All usable member locations create exactly one polygon.
  • Distant members never create sub-outages or extra published polygons.
  • polygon_area_km2 is the geodesic area.
  • unhealthy_device_count equals the complete frozen device list.
  • located_unhealthy_device_count records members usable for geometry.
  • healthy_device_count uses non-members that were live in the same pre-onset window and remained reachable.
  • Healthy and unhealthy counts use distinct devices.

Quality stop: if one reliable polygon cannot be built, return a geometry-quality failure. Never split the outage.

6. Internal evidence

Signals support triage but never alter outage_id or the output grain.

Cross-CSP correlation is evidence, not proof. Trust it only where CSPs have independent fibre and upstream paths.

7. Revision behaviour

  • Run at detection and refresh while the outage is open.
  • Increment revision and set revised_at.
  • Allow duration, contacts, and restoration to change cause or confidence.
  • Keep the current published row keyed only by outage_id.
  • Finalise on restoration and make updates idempotent.

Audience routing: Communication applies audience-specific thresholds. Labs supplies confidence and restoration class; it does not decide or send messages.

9. Labels and validation

  • Ship the feedback loop alongside v1.
  • Backfill historical pings, outages, and ISD contacts first.
  • Use neutral CSP confirmation as the strongest practical label.
  • Never connect confirmation to penalties, SLA scoring, or incentives.
  • Treat ticket volume as visibility evidence, not cause, unless reason text supplies cause.
  • Normalise contact rate against the locality baseline.
  • Keep UNKNOWN instead of forcing a guess.
  • Calibrate on held-out labels and beat the majority or “always power” baseline.
  • Pilot sentinels before deployment.

Ping reachability cannot prove physical root cause. If the classifier does not beat the baseline, retain the plumbing and collapse the unsupported distinction.

11. Minimum handoff

  • Narrow Detection GET with stable ID and frozen membership.
  • Portfolio-wide historical ping access.
  • Event-time coordinates and CSP mappings.
  • Concurrent-outage visibility.
  • Historical ISD contacts and CSP-confirmation path.
  • Agreed taxonomy, restoration classes, and revision cadence.

Fail closed: stop classification when keys, membership, timestamps, or location coverage fail validation. Never guess missing facts.
